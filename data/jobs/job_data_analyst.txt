Data Analyst
Company: Insightly Analytics (fictional)
Location: Bengaluru / On-site
Experience: 2-4 years

About the role
Insightly is hiring a Data Analyst to turn business data into clear dashboards and recommendations. You will work with product, sales and operations teams.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL queries to extract and analyze data from large databases.
- Build interactive dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
- Clean, transform and validate data using Excel and Python (pandas).
- Run A/B tests and statistical analysis to support business decisions.
- Present insights and data stories to non-technical stakeholders.
- Maintain documentation of metrics and data definitions.

Required skills
- Strong SQL (joins, window functions, CTEs)
- Advanced Excel including pivot tables and lookups
- Power BI or Tableau
- Python with pandas and NumPy
- Statistics fundamentals
- Data visualization and communication skills

Good to have
- ETL experience
- Google Analytics
- Experience in retail or e-commerce
